near-zero primary-meter transition, a material stable provider-anchor advance,
and credit-count movement that separates grants and redemptions from automatic
resets. The known September 26 anchor remains the strongest scheduled-reset

appears with a grant time and expiry while the primary meter and anchor do not

Signals that failed or remain too sparse are promise wording without a later
state transition, universal wording without a recipient denominator,
notification text, issue volume, capacity errors, incident proximity,
original-versus-reply position, release volume, mirror text without canonical
validation, weekday, UTC hour, cooldown, and milestone context. One resolved
banked-grant episode is too small for a defensible probability or population
delivery estimate.

What changed since September 22 is specific. The promised Tuesday action was
identified as a banked-reset rollout, and one account received the credit about
two and a half hours later. No hard reset occurred. The next detection tests
are more recipient and nonrecipient bounds, grant-time spread, expiry
consistency, successful redemption, credit-count decrement, resulting
five-hour and weekly anchor changes, and any official correction to the stated
scope.
